# gstack reliability follow-ups (October 4, 2026): headroom, honest eval verdicts, last host gaps

## Implementation plan

### Context

Base: origin/main `2db0b3adc` (v1.91.19.0, #3025). The severe fix wave left follow-ups in TODOS.md. Its eight periodic censuses on the wave branch (37174266054 … 37198445662) are the evidence base for this plan:

- 0 of 8 censuses were green; 21 verdict reds in total (2-3 per census).
- By cause: 10 detectors, scripted actors or the CEO mode handoff (each repaired one at a time); 5 judge low samples; 3 provider-shaped events (one stream stall, two refusals); 2 fixture/product; 1 headroom.
- 27 of 28 failed trials are labelled `assertion`, including observer timeouts, so the reports themselves misdescribe what happened.
- At the measured rate (about 1.2% red per verdict across ~193 verdicts) an all-green census is about 7% likely. The lever is the per-verdict red rate, and the biggest single source is brittle detectors and scripted actors, not provider events.
- Slowest cases: plan-ceo-mode-routing up to 687 s and plan-mode-no-op 613 s; ship-docsync-completion at 88% and store at 79% of budget; census wall time 14-15 minutes against the ~10-minute target. review-army-perf (102-140 s of 300 s) and plan-design-review-plan-mode (142-240 s, no timeouts) are not headroom problems.
- In census 37198445662, 20 of the 21 workflow judges that score actionability sat at exactly 4.00 and none scored 5: text edits can remove low samples but cannot lift a mean that the rubric caps at 4.

### Release promises

- **P0. Census reds become rarer and honest.** The PR reports, per census, verdict reds by machine class and the expected all-green probability, for the 8 pre-PR censuses and every census on this PR. Every red class that recurred has a structural fix (replay corpus, structured signal or speedup), not a one-off spelling patch.
- **P1. The slowest cases get headroom.** Cases the headroom report flags above 75% of their armed budget are brought under it by doing less work; no budget is raised. The weekly `eval:pass-rates --gate` alarm (not a free check against a committed file, which could never fail on a code change) flags any case above 85%. The census wall target is 10 minutes, so the slowest gate slice must fall to about 450 s (today 711 s).
- **P2. Labels say what happened.** Every trial record keeps `failure_class` (`assertion | contract | timeout | infra`, which verdicts read) and its 300-character `error` field exactly as today, and gains optional, bounded fields: `failure_cause` (`contract`, `pre_turn_infra`, `api_error`, `refusal`, `provider_stall`, `session_timeout`, `observer_timeout`, `assertion`, `unknown`), `failure_cause_evidence`, and `failure_detail` (assertion `{expected, received}` or judge `{dimension, mean, samples, rationale}`, each part capped). A machine cannot tell a detector miss from a product miss, so there is no `detector` cause; that is a diagnosis written by a person. Verdict outputs (`panelVerdict`, `collector-outcomes.json`, lane exit codes, INFRA re-dispatch eligibility) are identical under EVAL_POLICY v1, proven by running main's and the branch's verdict code over the 8 baseline censuses' artifacts and by running a frozen copy of the v1 reader over new records. This PR is judged under v1.
- **P2b. A red points to its evidence and the next step.** Each red line in the census report and tracking issue shows the case and trial, the observed failure with its Expected/Received values (or, for a judge, the dimension, mean, samples and lowest-scoring rationale), the artifact that holds the transcript, and the next action: inspect and replay free first; a paid run only "after a repair", selecting just that case. Target: diagnosis from the report line alone in 5 minutes per red (today 15-20). Every model- or fixture-written string that reaches a public issue, PR comment or committed file passes one sanitizer: control characters stripped, mentions neutralized, a code fence longer than any backtick run, HTML escaped, length capped, and the repo's credential-scan patterns redacted (adversarial test: @mention, triple backtick, `</details>`, image link, `ghp_`/`sk-` token, 10 KB string).
- **P3. Every low judge sample has a cause.** Each low workflow-judge sample in recent censuses is traced to a skill-text fix or recorded as a density-only complaint; judges are first checked on deliberately bad control bundles to learn whether they can discriminate at all.
- **P4. The wave's small product gaps close** (Bun floor, make-pdf `$P`, the #2709 reap, /review's fingerprint under Codex's read-only `.git`, design model smoke, live Codex periodic case).

### Workstream 0 (first, free): evidence

- **0.1 Session ledger, headroom report and red ledger.** Runners (SDK, PTY, Codex) append one line per armed session to a small session ledger file in the trial's artifact dir (same pattern as `contract-violations.jsonl`): stable session key, the exact timeout value it armed, monotonic elapsed, end reason, and a compact liveness summary (longest in-request silence, last event kind, tool open at end). The record builders (including `junitCensus` for PTY and JUnit-only cases) attach it to trial records. A new module (not `eval-flake-rank.ts`) reports per case: elapsed vs armed budget per session (a case's headroom is its slowest session; timed-out samples are marked censored), the lane critical path from runner slice timings, and verdict reds by machine cause with per-case rates. Past censuses carry no session clocks, so history shows the case wall as a labelled upper bound and session headroom starts with the first census after this lands; below a minimum sample count a case is `insufficient`, never a pass. Reached as `bun run eval:pass-rates --headroom | --reds | --run <id>` with offline `--help`, unknown-flag and unknown-case rejection, `--case` applied to JSON and alarms, and the printed run/branch scope; `--run` downloads only the slices its red lines name. The all-green probability is Π(1-p_i) over per-case shrunk rates with an interval, labelled an approximation (independence is not proven).
- **0.2 Failure cause classifier (policy-neutral).** One pure, dependency-free `failureCauseOf()` beside `failureClassOf()` (not in `e2e-helpers.ts`, which imports `bun:test` and selection machinery), called by both record builders from runner observations and the session ledger; precedence contract > pre_turn_infra > api_error > refusal > provider_stall > session_timeout > observer_timeout > assertion > unknown; it never changes `failure_class`, and conflicting evidence yields the higher-precedence cause with both recorded: a PTY idle turn showing an `API Error:` panel records `api_error` with the panel line (the PTY runner's `observe()` already stops on it; other runners match); refusals are detected from the structured stop reason first, phrases last; a stream stall is recorded only when the session streams partial messages and no event of any kind, thinking deltas included, arrives for 120 s (pre-registered; the longest in-message silence across 50 captured messages on Claude Code 2.1.284 was 9.6 s) while a model request is in flight and no tool call, permission prompt, hook or subagent is outstanding. Sessions without partial messages are never classified as stalls. Partial messages are enabled for this, but only the compact liveness summary is kept (full diagnostics would roughly quadruple transcript size). `panelVerdict` and lane verdicts are unchanged. Replays: 37195203538 (refusals), 37174266054 and 37176837432 (timeouts), and 37186854666 (it ran without partial messages, so it must come out `session_timeout`); the `provider_stall` positive is a synthetic fixture with an injected silence; controls: long-thinking, hung-tool, permission-pending, hook-running and subagent-running transcripts are not `provider_stall`; a tool result quoting refusal text is not `refusal`; one fixture per runner (SDK, PTY, Codex, JUnit).

- **0.3 Report and tracking issue.** Expected/Received values (today `sanitizeTrialError` keeps only the first line, so 16 of 28 wave failures show nothing useful) and judge scores and rationale go into `failure_detail`, not `error`; every red names its artifact; `rerun:` becomes "after a repair:" and selects only that case; the weekly issue includes the full pass-rate alarm lines (today its grep keeps only the header), the red ledger and the all-green probability (a documented formula with a command anyone can rerun); branch-dispatched censuses no longer post to or close main's tracking issue (both steps gated on `refs/heads/main`, same in evals-marathon.yml), and the periodic workflow's concurrency group is scoped by branch so a branch census no longer cancels main's; branch and run identity are passed explicitly instead of `git rev-parse --abbrev-ref HEAD`.
- **0.4 "A census went red" guide.** A short page linked from the report, the issue and CONTRIBUTING: prerequisites, the exact inspect and replay commands, expected output, what unknown data means, and when a paid run is allowed (only after a repair, per TESTING_INTERNALS). Durable evidence (the baseline red ledger, judge diagnoses) is committed under `docs/evals/`; scratch stays in `.context/`.

### Workstream A: headroom on the cases the report flags (P1)

- **A1. ship-docsync completion (88%) and store (79%).** Diagnose from the stored transcripts first. If the parent's reading of release-file contents dominates, the parent stops reading them and recomputes hashes with `bin/gstack-docs-candidate` (cheap, no model reading), comparing them with the child's claimed hashes; a mismatch stays a hard failure. Any change to the child's result contract updates the parser, child, actor and parent together, defines the hash algorithm and file set, and keeps negatives for stale results, missing files and unreported changes.
- **A2. plan-mode-no-op (432-613 s; likely the 711 s critical-path gate slice) and plan-ceo-mode-routing (687 s)**: time-boxed to the stored transcripts, report a per-phase breakdown (preamble, Step 0, model latency, tool time) first, then cut work in the skill or fixture; if model latency dominates, it becomes a named item rather than a cut that weakens the workflow.
- **A3.** Any other case the 0.1 report puts above 75%. review-army-perf and plan-design-review-plan-mode get no work unless the report flags them.
- **A4. Fresh critical journey.** The marathon lane runs the existing fresh /plan-eng-review case (plan-eng-review-artifact-full) on the PR head; time to the first question and to the written test plan are reported in the PR (non-blocking lane). A fresh /ship marathon case would be new harness work and goes to TODOS.

### Workstream B: robust detectors and actors (the largest red class)

- **B1. Replay corpora for repeat offenders.** shared-libs-plan-callers, plan-ceo-section-loading, auto-decide-preserved, plan-design-review-plan-mode and ship-docsync-late-result each get the stored passing and failing captures still downloadable (inventory first; artifacts expire after 30-90 days, and expired ones are listed as unavailable) replayed through their detector or actor in the free suite (≥1 pass and ≥1 fail each). Entries are minimal, redacted excerpts (the detector's input slice, size-capped, credential-scanned in CI), pinned to the series identity they came from, with stale entries reported for refresh. Each corpus entry records its original census outcome, the expected detector/actor result and a one-line justification, so a fixed false red is an expected pass with its history kept, and genuine negatives stay refused.
- **B2. Structured signals before phrases.** Where a skill already writes a structured record (decision ledgers, review logs, handoff helper output, `VERDICT:` lines), valid current structured evidence (right session, after the action, not copied fixture input) is authoritative; phrase detectors run only when no structured evidence exists. Where the contract is user-visible output (a question shown, text rendered), the behavioral witness stays required and the record only locates it. Audit every periodic and gate detector for phrase-only checks and convert the ones with a structured source.
- **B3. Floor runner TTY preview** (TODOS P2): accept collapsed context rows and gutterless wraps in `currentEditPreview`, still compared against exact source lines; replay the screen from PR run 37176835584.
- **B4. `--case` selects loop-registered cases** (coverage audits, TPA, plan-mode-no-op) and an unknown id exits non-zero before any process starts.
- **B5. Pre-push hook hang behind shard neighbors**: reproduce with the failing plan under xvfb and diagnose first; fix if the cause is in scope, otherwise a named open item.

### Validation

- Each fix has a free regression test that fails on main; detector and classifier changes replay captured transcripts with negative controls.
- Paid runs are pre-registered at the gate as an inventory: one diagnostic run per concrete repair of a `rule` case (never on unchanged inputs), a 3-trial panel for a changed `behavior` case, one 3-sample panel per changed judge and per C1 calibration set, the new E5/E6 cases once each to prove wiring, then the PR lane once on the frozen head (full gate, ~106 cases and 24 judges, because `engines.bun` and new files force full fallback), one periodic census on the frozen head, and the marathon run. Diagnostic runs never form a verdict.
- Free suite once on Ubicloud at the end; PR gate lane; one periodic census on the frozen head.
- Each workstream names the docs it updates (TESTING_INTERNALS, troubleshooting, the new red-census guide, CONTRIBUTING, AGENTS.md, README, TODOS.md). The 75%/85% thresholds and the stall window are named constants in one module; per-case exemptions go through a list with reason and expiry, never a raised budget.
- **Done when** PR CI is green and the census on the frozen head has every red either machine-classified INFRA under the approved policy or listed as a named red with diagnosis in the PR and TODOS.md. A repair made after that census needs a fresh census on the new head (TESTING_INTERNALS:407-411). "Diagnosis complete" is not "release accepted": under v1, an INFRA-only census may be re-dispatched once; any other red left unresolved needs Garry's explicit exception before merge. The PR body carries the red-class table, the all-green probability before/after, and a "diagnosable from the report line alone" column.
