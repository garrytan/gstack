**`/plan-ceo-review` explains its review-mode recommendation by what that mode does that the next-closest mode wouldn't.** It used to justify the pick by the plan's category, for example "the plan fits no clear category", which tells you nothing about why that mode is the better choice. Now the reason names a fact from your plan and what the recommended mode does with it that the nearest other mode wouldn't. In 10 runs on the AskUserQuestion format eval, every recommendation passed the substance check, against 7 of 10 before.
