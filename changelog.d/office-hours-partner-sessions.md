- **/office-hours asks for ground truth first.** Every startup session opens with one question: has it produced one real outcome yet, a paying user or someone who used it this week unasked? Founders with no outcome yet, and anyone who picks the new **Pivot** goal, get a one-page decision memo instead of a design doc. The memo has an evidence ladder, a conversation quota with the math, a payment test and a pivot ceiling check. Memos are saved apart from design docs (`*-memo-*.md`, repo copy in `docs/memos/`), so plan reviews never read one as a design.
- **Every startup session ends with a dated test.** The assignment carries a date and a number, and the doc or memo carries a KILL/CONTINUE or SUCCESS line. The approved line is recorded, and the next session on that project opens with it once the date has passed (`gstack-developer-profile --kill-check <slug>`). Customer names and prices stay in the private `~/.gstack` copy; repo copies use roles or initials. Builder mode is unchanged.
