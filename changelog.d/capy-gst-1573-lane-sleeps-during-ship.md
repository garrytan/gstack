### Changed

- **Captain docs: a lane sleeps while /ship runs.** `docs/captain.md` (the lane's /ship sequence), `captain/templates/brief.md` and the Capy gstack skill's "Sleep while /ship runs" bullet now say that after dispatching /ship, the lane ends its turn and wakes only when /ship completes, holding no pull request watch meanwhile; that one wake relays READY, and after READY the lane handles pull request events as before. Each READY duty has a named owner so `train build` never refuses a pin with `QUEUE_NOT_READY` for a step nobody ran: /ship owns the ship receipt and the pregate record behind the `PREGATE:` line; the lane owns `gstack-ship-queue ready --screen|--no-screen` and its notes, the decision-trail audit and the `Attention` section. The tradeoff is that a red check that lands while /ship runs waits for /ship to finish instead of being fixed in parallel.
