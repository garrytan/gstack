- **The design lint can now check one section of a running app screen for crowded rows, help text jammed under a list, notes as loud as names, and small tap targets.** It is render-only and opt-in: `--url <url>` (one state each) or `--state-file <states.json>`, a required `--within <selector>` matched through open shadow roots, `--viewport WxH` (1440x900 and 375x812 by default), `--wait-for`, `--waivers <file>` and `--verbose`. Five new rules at ask report only under `--scope app`: `row-crowding`, `copy-flush-under-list`, `note-in-row-weight`, `small-hit-area` and `section-overflow`, each finding with its state, viewport, a shadow-piercing locator, the measured px and the threshold. On gstack's replica of the qm#3186 Sharing panel it reports the four defect rules; on the fixed copy, the negative controls and a 12-page false-positive corpus it reports none. A `LINT_APP: verdict=<clean|open|incomplete> code=…` line follows `LINT_SUMMARY`, and the exit is 0 clean, 1 open, 4 incomplete (no browser, unreachable, timeout, not ready, state mismatch, no match, closed shadow root, probe error, missing stylesheet), 3 usage. URLs are redacted in every output and probe failures write a private trace file. See docs/design-lint.md "App profile (--scope app)".
- **Behavior change for every caller: `gstack-design-lint --url` with no browser now exits 4.** It prints `gstack-design-lint: RENDER_UNAVAILABLE: nothing was measured at <url> (<reason>) | fix: gstack-browser-ensure, then rerun` on stderr instead of passing with exit 0. `--render` over files without a browser is unchanged. `--within`, `--viewport`, `--wait-for`, `--waivers` and `--state-file` without `--scope app` exit 3, and the usage text gains the app line. Page and component output and exit codes are otherwise byte-identical (test/design-lint-scope-guard.test.ts).
