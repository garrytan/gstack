**New /ci-audit skill: measure where a repo's GitHub Actions time goes and get fixes ranked in minutes and dollars (GSTA-204).** Agents push, wait for CI and push again, so CI wall time limits the loop. Spotting why took a hand-written script each time. `/ci-audit` runs one program, `gstack-ci-audit`, that splits every sampled job into waiting for a machine, setup and running, measures push to all checks green per PR head, checks the nine lessons from Owen Gretzinger's 40-minutes-to-2 write-up, and prices the minutes from dated rows. It changes nothing unless you approve a fix, and `compare` prints before and after once the fix's CI has run. On gstack it reproduces GSTA-203's hand audit on the same 150 Free Tests runs: every job's wait p50/p90 matches, and job p50 is within 1 s.

### Added
- **`bin/gstack-ci-audit run`** (lib/ci-audit/): push to all checks green over all-green, first-attempt PR heads (cancelled, superseded, rerun, unsettled and window-edge heads are left out and counted), time to first failure, per-job waiting/setup/running with n, and cost from billed minutes scaled by per-workflow run counts (GitHub's runs list stops at 1,000 runs; the report says when the timing window is shorter). Ubicloud is priced by the VM family read from sampled logs, and cache hit rate is claimed only from restore lines in logs (`--logs N`, at most 20). Exit codes 0 complete, 1 usage, 3 unavailable, 4 partial. Errors are one `CI_AUDIT: unavailable <detail> (<code>)` line; ten `ci_audit_*` codes in `lib/gate-outcomes.ts` with docs/troubleshooting.md sections.
- **`gstack-ci-audit ship-check`**: a few-second, report-only check for /ship. It checks CI-shaping files in the diff against the lessons, and with `--pr` compares the PR's previous settled push with up to 40 comparable all-green PR heads. "Slower" means above their p95 and at least `ci_audit_slower_min_s` (default 120) over their median; under 8 comparable heads it prints `unknown thin baseline`. About 6 GitHub calls, 10 s deadline. `gstack-config set ci_audit_ship off` turns it off. (The /ship step that calls it lands separately.)
- **Saturday audit**: `lib/ci-audit/templates/ci-audit-weekly.yml` runs the report every Saturday at 15:00 UTC and `gstack-ci-audit publish` keeps one "Weekly CI audit" issue current. Only a complete audit replaces the issue or closes it; a partial or failed one comments and keeps the last findings.
- **`--record` / `--fixture`** (or `GSTACK_CI_AUDIT_FIXTURE`) save and replay GitHub responses with allowlisted fields only, plus the recording's clock; `test/fixtures/ci-audit/gstack` is a recorded gstack window.
- **Paid case `ci-audit-planted`** (gate, behavior) runs /ci-audit on a synthetic repo with three planted problems and two decoys; `ci-audit-planted-no-gstack` (marathon lane) gives the same task and data with no skill text.
- **docs/ci-audit.md**, catalog rows, and the doctor's `ci-audit` row.
