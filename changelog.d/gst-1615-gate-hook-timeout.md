- **The /investigate and /plan-ceo-review gates no longer pass a slow scan unchecked.** Their hooks were registered with a 10 second timeout while `GSTACK_SKILL_GATE_DEADLINE_MS` could set a longer gate deadline, and Claude Code kills a hook at its timeout and lets the stop through with no marker. `gstack-skill-gates settings` and `./setup` now register a timeout derived from the longest deadline plus a margin (25 seconds), and a deadline above 20 seconds is clamped to 20. A killed hook still cannot fail closed; docs/skill-gates.md#hook-timeout says why. Re-run `./setup` to update an existing registration. (GST-1615)
