- **gstack agents now open every status report with what failed, what they didn't verify and what else they changed.** The shared closing rule used to ask for "a few short lines". Now a report stays under 250 words and opens with three lines, `Failed or skipped:`, `Unverified:` and `Also changed:`, each naming every item or saying none. Lists longer than 10 get the top 10 and a count of the rest. Capy crews follow the same shape (factory.md point 10, the lane report and READY templates). On a 40-task dev split with Claude Opus 5.5, this wording raised the share of key facts disclosed in a report's first 250 words from 0.442 to 0.520 against the old closer, and in Capy lane reports from 0.552 to 0.604. Those are dev numbers, and the sealed held-out read is still to come (GST-1455).
