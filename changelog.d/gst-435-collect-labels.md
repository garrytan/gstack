**/ship's review collection no longer turns a reviewer's "no CRITICAL" summary into a CRITICAL finding.** In the HARN-15 runs (harness-evals #152 and the ship2 pass) the Red Team and fix-check readers opened with lines like "I found three INFORMATIONAL issues and no CRITICAL ones" and "there's nothing at CRITICAL". `collect` stored each of those lines as a CRITICAL finding, and `judge` would not let the lead drop it, so a clean pass read as blocked. The same happened to the fix-check closing line "Neither finding is CRITICAL." on gstack-evals#94. What reviewers see is unchanged, and every brief renders byte for byte as before.

### Fixed
- **A closing summary drops its negated label, and nothing else does.** A sentence that as a whole matches one of a short list of summary templates, such as "I found three INFORMATIONAL issues and no CRITICAL ones", "there's nothing at CRITICAL", "Neither finding is CRITICAL", "Neither fix is CRITICAL", "Nothing in this diff is CRITICAL" or "No CRITICAL or High findings to report", no longer counts as CRITICAL. A sentence that names a file:line, uses an exception or contrast word (except, besides, apart from, other than, but, however, only, though), runs over 100 characters or sits in a table row keeps every label. So "Nothing else is CRITICAL except the token leak in log.ts:3.", "No input validation is CRITICAL (upload.ts:4)" and "Nothing guards the write path the failure of which is CRITICAL" still collect as CRITICAL. A phrasing outside the templates, like "This is not CRITICAL.", also keeps its label, and lead judgment folds it. A kept finding never takes a weaker label than the strongest one left on its line, so the table row "| 1 | `CRITICAL` | High | token leak in log.ts:3 |" stays CRITICAL. The native adversarial voice's FIXABLE still collects as CRITICAL.
- **A list item whose label first appears in an indented later paragraph collects as that item.** The ship2 native voice wrote "4. **The host lock is not atomic** (`qm-stack.ts:66-76`)", two detail bullets, and then an indented "Fix: … FIXABLE. Severity: Low." paragraph. The finding was stored as the Fix paragraph alone, without the line that says what it is. It now starts with the item's first line. A labeled item's own labeled later paragraph is still its own finding.
- **No blocker is lost.** Main's parser and this one were run over 2,250 recorded reader outputs: 25 raw outputs from the GSTA-189 and GSTA-196 replays, the 2,172 finding records from arms C6, L6, A3668 (both suites), G1 and L, and 53 HARN-15 and test-fixture outputs. None of the 260 records matched to a judged blocker changes, and no arm record changes at all. All 24 changes are HARN-15 or GEVA-77 fixture outputs: 18 summary lines drop from CRITICAL to INFORMATIONAL, the GEVA-77 "Neither finding is CRITICAL." line collects nothing, and 5 native findings gain their titles.
