**/ship's review collection no longer turns a reviewer's "no CRITICAL" summary into a CRITICAL finding.** In the HARN-15 runs (harness-evals #152 and the ship2 pass) the Red Team and fix-check readers opened with lines like "I found three INFORMATIONAL issues and no CRITICAL ones" and "there's nothing at CRITICAL". `collect` stored each of those lines as a CRITICAL finding, and `judge` would not let the lead drop it, so a clean pass read as blocked. The same happened to the fix-check closing line "Neither finding is CRITICAL." on gstack-evals#94. What reviewers see is unchanged, and every brief renders byte for byte as before.

### Fixed
- **A severity word that a negator directly scopes is no label.** "not CRITICAL", "isn't really High", "no CRITICAL or High findings", "found nothing CRITICAL", "nothing at CRITICAL", "Neither finding is CRITICAL", "none of which is High" and "below High" no longer count. The negation has to apply to the label itself, and a negated subject counts only when it names the review's own findings ("Neither finding", "Nothing here", "none of which"): "No input validation is CRITICAL (upload.ts:4)", "Nothing guards the write path the failure of which is CRITICAL" and "None of the writes are atomic and are CRITICAL (queue.ts:78); High confidence" still collect as CRITICAL. A table row is never read for negation. A kept finding never takes a weaker label than the strongest one left on its line, so the table row "| 1 | `CRITICAL` | High | token leak in log.ts:3 |" stays CRITICAL. The native adversarial voice's FIXABLE still collects as CRITICAL.
- **A list item whose label first appears in an indented later paragraph collects as that item.** The ship2 native voice wrote "4. **The host lock is not atomic** (`qm-stack.ts:66-76`)", two detail bullets, and then an indented "Fix: … FIXABLE. Severity: Low." paragraph. The finding was stored as the Fix paragraph alone, without the line that says what it is. It now starts with the item's first line. A labeled item's own labeled later paragraph is still its own finding.
- **No blocker is lost.** Main's parser and this one were run over 2,250 recorded reader outputs: 25 raw outputs from the GSTA-189 and GSTA-196 replays, the 2,172 finding records from arms C6, L6, A3668 (both suites), G1 and L, and 53 HARN-15 and test-fixture outputs. None of the 260 records matched to a judged blocker changes. In the arms, two INVESTIGATE findings, each recorded in both arm G1 and arm L, lose a phantom label. One is a CRITICAL from "no CRITICAL" inside its sentence, and the other is a High from "resolves below High". Every other change is a HARN-15 or GEVA-77 summary line that drops from CRITICAL to INFORMATIONAL or to nothing, or a native finding that gains its title. The replay ran before review narrowed the negated-subject form, and that change only keeps more labels.
