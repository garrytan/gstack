- **A Captain-granted fix round after a fix-check is planned, not hand-run.** After round 2's fix-check and one granted fix-check, an owner who granted one more round at the last fix-check's stop head got `NEXT: stop` from `gstack-review-plan`, because in the loop a fix-check never earned another. The lane ran the fix-check by hand on a fix-range token, and finish printed `FIX_CHECK: not counted (fix-range pass; only a later full pass can clear review)` (gstack-evals#254). A new recorded grant naming the last fix-check's commit (`--range-base`) now plans one more scoped fix-check, the next link of the fix-check chain finish already counts; the previous link's grant (one recorded before a later logged fix-check) is refused and a grant at any other commit still stops, so one grant buys one link.
