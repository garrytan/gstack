**A first version's plan can say the owner ruled something out, and that line now counts only when he actually said it.** Under `### Owner's words`, a quote's line can be `ruled out <date>: "<ruling>"`. When one of the owner's recorded replies holds the ruling (three or more words, not just the quote repeated), the ruling beats a reviewer. A finding that says the plan does less than his words becomes one item recommending his ruling, and a cut of those words stays a cut. When no recorded reply holds it, the approval gate asks him once, showing the ruling and any finding side by side and recommending the ruling. A finding's twin from the other reviewer that doesn't name the quote now joins the same item instead of becoming a separate take-the-change proposal, and public exports redact the ruling like the quote.
