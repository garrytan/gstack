**The destructive-command check asks before two more kinds of delete outside the project, and three force-push spellings no longer slip past it.** A relative delete after `cd` into a system folder (`cd /etc/nginx && rm -f sites-enabled/default`) and removing a device node under `/dev` (`rm -f /dev/loop0`, also inside a loop) now ask, like their absolute-path forms already did. A force push to main is no longer read as a dry run when the `-n` is really the value of `-o`/`--push-option` (`git push -f -o -n origin main`), when `--push-option` is abbreviated (`--push`), or when the flags sit in a group with a digit (`-f4`, `-4f`, `-fo-n`). Deletes after a `cd` into `/tmp` or a project subfolder, `/dev/shm` files, output sent to `/dev/null`, and real dry runs are unchanged.
