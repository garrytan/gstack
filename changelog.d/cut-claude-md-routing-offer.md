**gstack no longer offers to add a `## Skill routing` section to your project's CLAUDE.md.** The prompt-routing hook, on by default, now gets the right skill started on its own: in a preregistered test on Claude Opus 5.5, it loaded the bug-fix skill in 45 of 60 and 48 of 60 unattended sessions, past the 39 of 60 that the CLAUDE.md section managed, while sessions without gstack loaded it in none ([results](https://github.com/garrytan/gstack-evals/tree/main/wiki/board/2026-11-21-capy-b1e-matcher-fix-results)). A section an older gstack added stays exactly as you left it and keeps working; gstack never edits it, and you can delete it. The `routing_declined` setting, which only answered that offer, is gone.
