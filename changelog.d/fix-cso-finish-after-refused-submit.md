**/cso keeps an audit open after a refused submit.** If the helper refused a submission (for example `exclusions must be an array`), `finish` used to close the audit anyway, and the corrected resubmit was then refused because the audit was no longer running, so the evidence was lost. Now `finish` says the last submit was refused and asks for a corrected one; it closes without that evidence only once the audit deadline has passed, and records the refused submission as a gap.
