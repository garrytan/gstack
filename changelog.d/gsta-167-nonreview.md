**/ship's review collection reads reviewer replies the way they were meant: a reviewer that says it could not review no longer counts as clean, and a closing "nothing here is CRITICAL" no longer counts as a CRITICAL finding.** In the GSTA-196 replay a specialist replied in prose that the diff base did not exist and it was not returning NO FINDINGS. `collect` parsed that reply to zero findings, and `verify` marked the brief collected, so the review would have read clean from a reader that never saw the diff. What reviewers see is unchanged: every brief renders byte for byte as before.

### Fixed
- **`gstack-review-dispatch collect` needs the clean marker a reader's own brief asks for before it counts a reply with no findings.** That is `NO FINDINGS` alone on a line for the specialists, the batched specialist, the targeted reader, the Red Team and fix-check; the `Recommendation:` line for the outside voice; and either one for the native adversarial voice. A reply with no finding and no marker (a refusal, an empty reply, a reader that never got the diff) is recorded with `review: false`, prints `COLLECT_NOT_REVIEWED` and exits 1.
- **`gstack-review-dispatch verify` reports that reader's brief `NOT_COLLECTED` and its output `NOT_REVIEWED`, so VERIFY reads dirty.** When the output file is still there and unchanged, verify checks the marker in it again, so a ledger written before this change is judged by the same rule. The GSTA-189 replay's combined ledger still verifies clean, with five `NO FINDINGS` specialists and one outside voice ending in `Recommendation:`.
- **A severity word used as a word is no longer a finding.** A fix-check reader on gstack-evals#94 closed with "Neither finding is CRITICAL.", `collect` stored that line as a CRITICAL finding, and `judge` would not let the lead drop it. Now a label that is negated ("not CRITICAL", "no High findings", "Neither finding is CRITICAL"), used as a yardstick ("below CRITICAL"), quoted or listed as vocabulary ("Critical|High") is skipped, and so is a closing "Overall:" or "Summary:" line after the findings. A label the line opens with, a `Severity:` field, a table cell, and a label whose negation describes the defect ("Without auth this is CRITICAL") still count.
- **A paragraph finding keeps its detail bullets.** The GSTA-196 native adversarial voice wrote four findings, each with unlabeled bullets and then a `Severity:` line. Every bullet closed the open finding, so each `Severity:` line opened a new one: 4 findings collected as 8. They now collect as 4, each at the stronger of its two labels.
- **No blocker is lost.** Main's parser and this one were run over every recorded reader output: 25 raw outputs from the GSTA-189 and GSTA-196 replays and 2,172 finding records from arms C6, L6, A3668 (both suites), G1 and L. The 8 GSTA-196 lines merge into 4 findings, none at a weaker label. One INVESTIGATE finding, recorded in both arm G1 and arm L, loses a phantom CRITICAL ("no CRITICAL" inside its sentence), and another loses a phantom High ("resolves below High"). None of the 260 records matched to a judged blocker changes.
