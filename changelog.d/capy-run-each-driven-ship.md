- **A driven /ship now runs each phase as one command, counts its calls, and stops itself at 100 minutes.** `gstack-ship-phase run` runs a ship/DRIVER.md phase's commands in order, prints only their result lines, returns after 200 s while slow work keeps running, and on a rerun skips what already finished; on Capy, `gstack-ship-phase program` serves a checked-in program that also runs the review pass or the closing budget note in the same call. `gstack-ship-budget note` records the calls since the previous note, and `gstack-ship-receipt write --budget-record <rec>` puts the run's total in the receipt, so `SHIP_RECEIPT:` shows `calls=` (older receipts without it still validate and carry). Past 100 minutes, the note or the phase runner prints `STOP: past 100 minutes` and writes the head, review passes and next act to `stop.json`; `gstack-ship-budget resume` restarts the clock. The driver also prices waits by length. It keeps a 200 s keep-alive under about 80 minutes, uses one wait for anything longer, and never polls every 10-15 minutes. Measured conductors made 121 to 308 calls against the driver's 9 to 12 and 10 of 12 ran past the 100-minute line while these limits were prose.
