- **A reader's prose that only mentions a severity no longer stops /ship.** The review collector took a severity word anywhere in a reader's reply as a finding at that label. #4208's pass-2 red team returned `NO FINDINGS` but quoted `CRITICAL (the reviewer wrote …)` in a coverage note, and an alphaclaw fix-check wrote "No fix introduced a CRITICAL defect"; both collected as CRITICAL, judge may not drop a collected finding, and `gstack-review-plan` printed `NEXT: stop` (GST-2122). A severity word now rates a finding only through the finding's structure, as the reader briefs ask for it: a JSON object, a lead label, a label heading, a numbered finding heading, a `Severity` field, `Blocks: yes`, the native voice's FIXABLE or INVESTIGATE tag, or a file:line. Prose with none of these collects as an INFORMATIONAL note at most, and nothing in a reply that says `NO FINDINGS`. Structured findings, including malformed JSON and a structured finding beside `NO FINDINGS`, still collect at their label. The trade: a real defect a reader describes only in prose, with no file:line ("Neither fix clears the P0."), is now a note the lead may promote instead of a blocker.
