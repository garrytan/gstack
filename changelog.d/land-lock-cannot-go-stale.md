**A crashed `gstack-ship-queue land --pin` no longer blocks every other merge.** Its lock now records the process holding it and expires 90 minutes after its last step, so the next land takes over a lock whose land has exited, whose machine restarted, or that expired, and logs the takeover; a land that is still running is never interrupted. New `gstack-ship-queue count` prints how many finished PRs are waiting to merge, from one gh call, with a DRAIN line when more than 6 wait or one has waited a day. docs/ship-policy.md#land-lock, docs/ship-policy.md#revert-drill, crew/docs/captain.md#drain-the-queue
