- **`gstack-ship-receipt write` now says why a targeted record from a rewritten commit is refused.** When the commit the targeted reader read is not an ancestor of HEAD (usually after a rebase or a redone merge resolution), the RECEIPT_INVALID message now names that commit, says it is not an ancestor of HEAD, and says the record carries only from HEAD or an ancestor of it, even when the product patch is unchanged, so the targeted reader must run on HEAD. Before, it said the product changed after the read while printing two equal product patch ids, and lanes went looking for a product change that wasn't there (qm#2952). What passes and fails is unchanged. (GST-1700)
