- **A parallel shard's overrun is measured against its wall-time prediction.** `--shard-jobs k` splits a shard across k jobs, but the CI health summary still compared the shard's wall-clock `elapsedMs` with its serial `predictedMs`: a split shard looked faster than predicted, so a real overrun nothing warned about. The summary now divides the prediction by the shard's job count before comparing, `ciHealthSummary` takes the plan's `jobs`, and the warning names how many jobs ran in parallel, so a shard that genuinely outran its seed is reported under `--ci-verify` too. `docs/TESTING_INTERNALS.md` states the wall-time comparison.
