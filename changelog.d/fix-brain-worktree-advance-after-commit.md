- **gbrain now sees your newest gstack artifacts within minutes, not up to a day later.** gbrain indexes a separate snapshot of `~/.gstack`, and gstack used to move that snapshot forward at most once a day, at the start of a sync and before that sync's own commit. So the brain was always missing the artifacts gstack had just published, sometimes for more than a day. Now every sync moves the snapshot forward at its end, after its commit and any merge from the remote, whenever the snapshot is behind. A snapshot that can't be moved forward (for example because it has local changes) still warns only once a day.
