**The AskUserQuestion format eval scores its recommendation check with three judge samples instead of one.** One Haiku 5.5 sample on the same captured question scored 3 or 4 by chance, so the eval's verdict flipped run to run; it now gates on the median of three, like the AskUserQuestion matrix eval, and the judge's calibration test gains two real captured questions on either side of the pass line, so a future judge-model change that moves them fails that test.
