**A first version's public export now catches a short owner quote whose padding or quote marks are encoded.** `gstack-autoplan export --public` matched a short plan quote on the raw text only, so a copy that wrote the padding as `&nbsp;` or a zero-width space, the quote marks as `&quot;` or `\"`, or a space as `&#32;` kept the owner's words, and `validate --public` passed it. Short quotes are now matched on the same decoded text the long-quote scan reads, and the redaction still covers the original characters.
