- **QA captures on macOS no longer record a finished command as incomplete.** After a command exited, the cleanup kill of its process group could get `EPERM` from macOS while the group was being torn down, and `gstack-qa-evidence capture` then recorded `status: incomplete`, `exitCode: 2`. On macOS, an `EPERM` now makes the capture check the group directly and count it as gone only when no live member remains; a group that still has a live member, or a check that cannot run, still fails closed, and `EPERM` on other platforms fails closed as before. Reported with measurements by @vilano93 (#4166).
