- **`/ship` accept gates under a branch's own pregate policy when it only adds test lanes, says why a carried lane's test file is missing from CI's JUnit tree, and takes an owner-granted test-lane exception.** On gstack-evals #240 a PR that added a runtime file and its own lane line passed preflight under `--policy-from head`, then `gstack-ship-release accept` reran the pregate under the base policy and refused with `PREGATE_NO_LANE`. `accept`, and `finish` when it reruns the whole pregate, now pass `--policy-from head` when the branch's `.gstack/pregate.json` differs from the copy it forked from in nothing but added test lanes and `.gstack/generated.json` is unchanged, printing `POLICY: head …`; a removed or shrunk lane, or any other changed key, keeps the base policy with `POLICY: base … (the branch's copy <what differs> …)`. The same PR then stopped on `inventory_uncovered`: bun registers an imported test file's tests under the importer, so its JUnit suite never appears. The refusal now adds `CARRY_DIAGNOSIS: bun registers <imported> under <importer>; its JUnit suite won't appear`, and the owner can record `gstack-approvals lane-exception --head <pushed head> --files <the uncovered files> --reason … --words … --by …` (refused inside the lane's open /ship run, by gstack, or through `add`). `gstack-evidence record-ci` binds it only at that head for exactly those files, after a local preflight of them with the lane's `per_file` runner passes; `accept` prints `TEST_LANE_EXCEPTION:`, the receipt's `ci_carried` entry records the grant and the preflight, and `gstack-ship-receipt write --body` refuses a body without the `Test-lane exception: …` line. See docs/pregate.md#accept-head-policy and docs/ship-policy.md#test-lane-exceptions. (GST-1595)
