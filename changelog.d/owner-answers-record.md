- **A Captain can check an ask against the owner's earlier answers before sending it.** `gstack-owner-answers` keeps the owner's answers and standing yeses in one record, outside every repository: on Capy it is the owner's private Drive folder, which every thread the owner runs can read. `screen --ask-file` reads a Captain's draft ask and proposes leaving out only the lines that a verified, current standing yes in the owner's own words covers exactly. Every other line stays in, with any related answers shown beside it. That includes autoplan gate items, untyped or compound lines, and anything that always needs a fresh ask (product decisions, spend over a cap, access, publishing, thresholds, closing a contribution, destructive steps). A damaged, conflicting or unreadable record keeps every ask. Rows are one file each, written once; a row needs a second actor to verify its words before it can be taken, and the recorder can't verify its own. The file format and the two-week pilot are in docs/owner-answers.md.
