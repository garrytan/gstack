- **`/runaway-stopper` no longer flags lanes that follow the warm-wait rule.** `gstack-runaway scan` counted each 240-second warm-wait check-in as an empty wake, so a compliant lane waiting on CI could cross the 25% empty-wake warning. A turn whose only call re-issues the wait on the same operation, or a 240-second timer, within 30 minutes of the wait that started the run is now left out of the rate. A re-wait past 30 minutes, a different wait, or a turn that also reads status still counts. (GST-1597)
