### Fixed

- **/ship no longer closes a review pass before its second-model readers have run.** After the first readers finished, the review program printed `NEXT: judge (Phase 4)` and nothing more, even when the pass's adversarial and second-model readers were still waiting for their turn after the fixes. A lane could read that as the end of the pass, skip those readers, and only meet their findings on the last read-only pass, with no fix round left (gskills#54). The line now names the readers still owed (`NEXT: judge (Phase 4); still owed after its fixes: …`), `gstack-review-dispatch verify` reports the pass dirty with `VERIFY_OWED` until every reader it planned is collected, and `judge` refuses while a reader it already dispatched has not come back. Both commands check the pass's own manifest (beside the ledger, or named with `--manifest`) and refuse a pass whose manifest they cannot find.
