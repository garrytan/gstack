**Free Tests on pull requests starts its test shards one round of small jobs sooner.** The job that plans the shards now runs beside the reuse and docs-only checks instead of after them, so the shards no longer wait twice for a GitHub-hosted runner (that second wait was 21 seconds at the median and 6 minutes at p95 over the last two weeks). Every shard still waits for both checks, so a reused main push or a docs-only pull request still runs no shard, and the test-health and scorecard reports still leave reused main pushes out of their flake counts.
