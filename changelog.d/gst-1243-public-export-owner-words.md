**`gstack-autoplan export --public` no longer carries the owner's own words (GST-1243).** GEVA's public bundle (GEVA-225, gstack 1.93.34.0) kept all 38 `owner_reply` fields in decisions.jsonl, and a sealed reviewer file (`ceo-native.a5.bound.md`) quoted an owner reply word for word. GEVA redacted both by hand. Garry's rule: nothing a person wrote leaves without their click.

### Fixed
- **Dropped fields.** The public copy drops `owner_reply` from every decision row and from `run.json`'s gate, the `words` a carried gate reused (its `CARRIED:` line keeps `words=[owner words redacted]`), and the reason a human gave for `close --deferred --by human` (replaced by `[owner words redacted]` along with the gate titles and log lines that repeat it). The decision itself stays: `chosen`, the gate-grammar `reply`, `owner_mode` and `owner_reply_parsed`. An agent's own words (`answer_words`) and an agent's deferral reason stay too.
- **Redacted quotes.** Every exported file, sealed `*.bound.md` reviewer results included, is scanned for quotes of those words: any 8 consecutive words of a reply (the whole reply when it is shorter), compared without edge punctuation or case and across line breaks and `>` blockquote markers. Replies under 5 words or 20 characters ("yes", "do it") and replies that parsed as gate grammar are not scanned for, so common words are never redacted. Each quote becomes `[owner words redacted]`, prose without one keeps its bytes, and an `OWNER_WORDS: <file> dropped=<n> redacted=<m>` line names each changed file.
- **Refusal.** A quote inside plan.md code, which export never rewrites, stops the export with `ARTIFACT_OWNER_WORDS`, file:line and exit 3, and nothing is written. `gstack-artifact validate --public <dir> --private <run.json>` runs the same check. The error never prints the words.
