- **QA captures on macOS no longer report a successful command as incomplete.** After a command exited, the cleanup kill of its process group could get `EPERM` from macOS while the group was still being torn down, and `gstack-qa-evidence capture` then recorded `status: incomplete`, `exitCode: 2`. `EPERM` on macOS now counts as a group that is already gone; other errors and other platforms still fail closed.
