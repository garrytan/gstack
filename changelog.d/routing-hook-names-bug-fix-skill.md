- **The prompt-routing hook now names your installed bug-fix skill on bug reports, alone, and never puts /verify ahead of it.** A prompt that reads as a bug report (a crash, a wrong result, a hang, an exception name, "expected behavior", "fix ...") goes to the installed skill whose triggers claim bug fixing, such as prove-it's "fix this bug". A fixer whose trigger names its own target, such as "fix lint errors", claims no bug reports, and a request whose words match all of one of /review's or /qa's triggers keeps that skill. On B1's 60 in-sample bug-fix prompts the hook now names prove-it on all 46 bug reports; before, it named prove-it on 40 of the 60 and put /verify first on 7. Another installed skill's trigger now needs two content words, so one word such as "error" can no longer claim most bug reports, and the opt-in `GSTACK_SKILL_ROUTE_HINT=step` and `contract` styles quote only gstack's own skills.
