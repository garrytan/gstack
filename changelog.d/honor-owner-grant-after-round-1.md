- **An owner's grant of one scoped fix-check now works after round 1, and a grant row the review planner can't use says which field to fix.** On gbrain #8839 the owner granted one scoped fix-check instead of a second full review pass, but `gstack-review-plan --grant` only honored a grant after round 2, so it still printed `NEXT: full`. The lane ran the fix-check by hand, and Step 11.5 then refused with **Review records missing or mismatched**, because Step 11's record and the fix-check sat on different trees. Now `--pass 1 --last full --grant <id>` prints `NEXT: fix-check` in place of round 2's full or scoped pass. Step 11.5 binds that granted fix-check at its own tree when its range holds Step 11's commit. Passing the same grant again after the fix-check it bought plans as if no grant were given, so one grant still buys one fix-check. A refused grant row now names the one thing to fix: a row missing from this branch's record, an outcome other than approved, a row that says `review_loop_exception` instead of `review_loop_grant`, a sha this clone doesn't have, or a commit that isn't the stop head, in which case the message prints the head it expected. `review_loop_grant <sha> on #<n>` with a short or full sha binds.
