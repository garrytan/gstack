**A first version's ruled-out line now counts only against the one reply it cites.** The date in `ruled out <date>: "<ruling>"` names the owner's recorded reply by its record id, its time (`2026-11-18 09:20Z`), or its day when he sent only one reply that day. The ruling beats a reviewer only when that reply holds its words. A citation that names no reply or several, or a reply that doesn't hold the ruling, makes the approval gate ask him instead. Words from some other reply no longer count. Keeping his ruling also leaves the build with no task for the fix he turned down. A second reviewer's finding that names a different quote now stays on its own item instead of joining this one. (GST-3646)
