- **The free-CI shard-overrun warning now works on split shards.** `--ci-verify` compared a shard's wall time with its serial seed prediction, so at the CI default of 4 sub-shards per shard it only warned after about a 6x slowdown. The prediction is now divided by the shard's job count, and the warning says how many parallel jobs it ran across. Thanks to @indraoverflow (#4051, #3851).
