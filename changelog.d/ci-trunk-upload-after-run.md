**Pull requests no longer wait on the Trunk flaky-test upload, and the quick tier's flaky passes now reach Trunk.** The upload that sends Free Tests results to Trunk was a job inside Free Tests, so in about one pull request run in four it was the last job to finish: usually by a few seconds, but by about three minutes in the slowest twentieth of those runs and once by more than six. It now runs as its own workflow after Free Tests completes, outside the pull request's check list. The quick tier on the install-default Bun now keeps its JUnit reports too (`bun run test:quick --junit-dir <dir>` is new), and they go to Trunk as the `bun-default` variant, so a test that fails and then passes on its one retry there shows up in Trunk, not only in the flake ledger.
