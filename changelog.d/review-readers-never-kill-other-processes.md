- **/ship's review readers are now told never to stop processes they didn't start.** Every brief a /ship review pass sends to a reader now says that other runs share the machine, so the reader must not run `pkill`, `killall`, kill by name or pattern, or kill a process id its own command didn't start, whatever the rest of the brief asks. A red-team reader had run `pkill -x sleep` on a machine shared by several /ship runs and ended processes those runs had started. (GST-3360)
