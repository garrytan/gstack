**The prompt router stops sending feature requests to your bug-fix skill.** A request whose title asks for something new ("[Feature]: …", "Support …", "Add …") no longer counts as a bug report because its body mentions an error, unless the title itself names a defect, the body asks for a fix, or the body follows an issue template (steps to reproduce, expected or actual behavior).
