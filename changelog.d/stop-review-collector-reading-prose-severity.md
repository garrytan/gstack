- **A reader's clean reply, or a sentence denying a severity, no longer stops /ship.** The review collector took a severity word anywhere in a reader's reply as a finding at that label. #4208's pass-2 red team returned `NO FINDINGS` but quoted `CRITICAL (the reviewer wrote …)` in a coverage note, and an alphaclaw fix-check wrote "No fix introduced a CRITICAL defect". Both collected as CRITICAL, judge may not drop a collected finding, and `gstack-review-plan` printed `NEXT: stop` (GST-2122). Now a reply that says `NO FINDINGS` keeps only its structured findings, which still collect at their label. Those are JSON objects (malformed ones included), lead labels, label headings, numbered headings, `Severity` fields, `Blocks: yes`, the native FIXABLE or INVESTIGATE tag, and anything naming a file:line. Its other prose collects nothing. A denial that a change introduced a severity is a no-defect clause, including after a bold lead such as `**1. Coverage:**`. Other prose keeps its label as before, so a real prose blocker such as "Neither fix clears the P0." still blocks.
