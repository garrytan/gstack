**/ship no longer asks for an extra reader when you merge the base branch next to code that was already reviewed.** If merging main leaves your own added and removed lines, file modes and symlink targets exactly as they were reviewed, /ship and the receipt carry treat the head as reviewed and let CI on the merged head check it. A merge that splices main's change into the same lines you changed still gets one scoped reader, and a mode flip, a symlink retarget or a conflict resolution that edits your own lines still needs a full review. The receipt also no longer needs the docs, review-loop, issue or budget records to bind; older receipts that carry them still work.
