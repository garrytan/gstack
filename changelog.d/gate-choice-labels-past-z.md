**/autoplan approval asks with more than 26 choices now label them AA, AB and so on instead of punctuation.** The ask lettered its choices one character at a time, so choice 27 became `[`, choice 33 became `a` (the same answer as choice 1, since answers are read in any case), choices 63 to 95 were control characters that don't print, and later ones were stray symbols. Labels now run A to Z and then on like spreadsheet columns (AA, AB, … ZZ, AAA), every one capital letters and none repeated, so a reply like "AA1" names exactly one choice next to "A1". (#4769)
