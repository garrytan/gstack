**`gstack-gbrain-mcp-verify` no longer misreads a large or slow-to-write SSE reply as broken.** It checked the first line through `echo | head -1 | grep -q` under `pipefail`, which fails with SIGPIPE when a reader exits early, so a valid reply could end the probe with jq's exit 5. Two gate-ahead preparations for one PR no longer fail each other with `failed to read .git/worktrees/worktree/commondir: Success`: the worktree adds and removals of the version stamp and the gate-ahead queue now take one lock per repository.
