- **The prompt-routing hook no longer sends ordinary bug reports to /qa (GST-3123).** After the bug-report matching fix, /qa's "find bugs on site" and "test the site" triggers matched 3 of 60 real bug reports on everyday words: "cannot find" plus "fails", or "call site" plus "regression tests" far apart. Two of a trigger's words now count only when one of them is specific, or when short everyday words sit within 8 words of each other. Real requests such as "please test the site" and "find bugs on my site" still route to /qa, and the 40 of 60 bug reports that match a bug-fix trigger still do.
