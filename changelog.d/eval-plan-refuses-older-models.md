**`/eval-plan` refuses gpt-4o and older-generation models in every role a plan chooses.** A preregistration's arms, judge, readers and simulators, and every dry-run and pilot arm, are checked against gpt-4o (any variant) and the models `lib/pricing.ts` marks as replaced by a newer generation; each refusal names the model, its role and the current replacement. A competitor system tested as its vendor ships it keeps its built-in model through a recorded `vendor-default:` line. Plans frozen on or after 2026-11-22 also check a model judge against at least 100 hand labels instead of 40. gstack's own eval warmup, distill and recommendation-judge defaults and five canary tests move from Claude Haiku 4.5 to Haiku 5.5.
