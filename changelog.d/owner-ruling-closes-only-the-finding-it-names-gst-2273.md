- **An owner's ruling can now close a blocking finding on /ship's second full review pass and on the cross-model review step, as the review policy already promised, and it closes only the one finding it names.** Before, only the narrow re-check after a fix read the owner's rulings, so a pass held open by a finding the owner had accepted stayed unverified, and a cross-model finding that no review row recorded counted as unrecorded (garrytan/gstack#4248, #4266, #4257). The owner now rules one finding with `gstack-approvals rule --id <pass dir>/<id> --head <stop head>`. That ruling closes the finding with that id on the /ship review row that read that head, when the ruling came after the read ended; `gstack-review-read` then reads the pass `CURRENT`, and `gstack-review-scope summary` counts the finding as a noted disclosure. A finding only the cross-model step's log holds closes only when the run's newest review row read that head and the whole step ran during that read. Nothing else closes: rulings by fingerprint (which keep their meaning on the re-check after a fix), a ruling at another head or made before the read ended, an id two findings share, and any gate finding, including one whose cross-model line names a gate even when the review row left that out.
