**`gstack-ship-queue ready` no longer goes silent for minutes on a long merge queue.** Its trial merges against other open pull requests used to fetch each missing pull request head on its own, one after another, allowing 120 s per fetch, and printed nothing until the end (gbrain #10011). Ready now fetches every missing head in one `git fetch`, prints a progress line before it starts, and stops after 60 s (`GSTACK_TRIAL_FETCH_TIMEOUT_MS` changes the limit). When the limit runs out, ready refuses and names each `refs/pull/<n>/head` it was waiting on, instead of printing READY with that trial merge missing. (GST-3460)
