- **An agent's own words no longer settle a user challenge at the /autoplan gate.** `gstack-autoplan answer --words-by agent:<id>` let a Captain choose a `uc` item (a user challenge both reviewers raised, or a round-cap approval) on its own authority, though only the owner may settle one. Such a reply, whether `rec`, `as-is` or a `uc` token, is now refused with `OWNER_REPLY_REQUIRED`, naming the item and asking for the owner's words, and the gate stays open. The agent can still answer the `d` items in its own words and leave the `uc` items to the owner. The rule that agent words are never the owner's now lives in one place, shared by the answer path, carry-forward and plan-sha citations.
