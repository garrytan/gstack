- `/ship`'s review collector no longer turns a severity word in a negation into a finding. A fix-check reader that closed with "found nothing CRITICAL" was recorded as a CRITICAL finding with no file or line, and the judge can't drop a finding, so the run carried a phantom blocker that could stop the review loop at its cap (GST-766, from GEVA-92 on gstack-evals #108). A label after "no", "nothing", "not", "none", "zero", "without" or "never", and labels listed with it ("no High or Critical"), now count as no finding. A label stated as a finding is collected as before.
