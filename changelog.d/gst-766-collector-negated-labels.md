- `/ship`'s review collector no longer turns a severity word in a negation into a finding. A fix-check reader that closed with "found nothing CRITICAL" was recorded as a CRITICAL finding with no file or line, and the judge can't drop a finding, so the run carried a phantom blocker that could stop the review loop at its cap (GST-766, from GEVA-92 on gstack-evals #108). A label right after "no", "nothing", "not", "none", "zero", "without" or "never" (optionally "new", "remaining" or "other", and through Markdown emphasis or brackets), and a label joined to it by "or", now count as no finding. A label that opens a field ("High: …") is still collected, and a line that names a file:line keeps its labels when all of them are negated, so no located defect is dropped.
