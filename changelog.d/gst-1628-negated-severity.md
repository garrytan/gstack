(gstack-evals#247) and "Nothing reaches the CRITICAL floor." (#4055) as CRITICAL findings, because its denial grammar knew only "is", so `blocking_found` carried a phantom that lead judgment was not allowed to lower. A rating verb (rises to, reaches, meets, qualifies as, would rise to) now denies the way "is" does: such a closing sentence collects at INFORMATIONAL, and on a line that also names a file:line it is a summary candidate that lead judgment may lower with a reason.
