**Plan reviews no longer read a /design-review audit summary as the branch's design doc.** After a design review, `/autoplan`, `/plan-ceo-review` and `/plan-eng-review` could pick up the one-line `…-design-audit-…md` pointer, because `gstack-design-doc-find` matched it as a design doc. The finder now skips audit summaries the way it already skips decision memos, and still finds the real design doc, including on a branch whose name contains "design-audit". Reported by @robbiesierra in #4615.
