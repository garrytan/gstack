- **A clean Codex outside review that signs off with a sentence about its checks now reads `VERDICT: clean`.** Since #3304 only a review's last sentence (or its Recommendation because-clause) can conclude it, so a reply such as "No actionable regressions were identified in the diff. Tests, lint, and build all pass." read `VERDICT: unverified` / `REASON: untagged_review`, and /ship and /review reported missing outside coverage for a review that ran and found nothing. Closing sentences that only report passing checks are now skipped when the conclusion is read, and "The changes introduce no definite, actionable regressions." counts as a no-findings statement held to the whole-change scope rule. It stays fail-closed: a check sentence that quotes code, fails, is cut off, continues a wrapped line or follows a colon is never skipped, skipping one never makes a scoped claim the reply's only sentence, a clause joined after the whole-change scope by a comma, dash, slash, bracket or a word such as "and", "so" or "while" ("No issues in the diff, while the change disables auth.") is never a conclusion, and a review with findings is never read as clean. See docs/troubleshooting.md#outside-review-verdict. (#3469, GST-1803; design and tests from #3641 by @vilano93)
