**/ship no longer asks for an extra reader when you merge the base branch next to code that was already reviewed.** If merging main leaves your own added and removed lines, file modes and symlink targets exactly as they were reviewed, /ship treats the head as reviewed and lets CI on the merged head check it. A merge that splices main's change into the same lines you changed, or that has to choose the order of your inserted lines against lines main inserted at the same spot, still gets one scoped reader, and a mode flip, a symlink retarget or a conflict resolution that edits your own lines still needs a full review.
