- **The Ubicloud runner no longer destroys a VM another caller took under the same name.** With an explicit `-n` name, a second caller could take the name in the second between our name check and our create. If our create's 409 answer was lost, `up` found that caller's VM by name, adopted it, and its teardown destroyed it. Now a VM found after an unanswered or 5xx create is adopted with teardown off. Teardown turns on only once this call's own ssh key opens the VM; keys from the ssh agent or ssh config don't count. If the key never does (within `UBI_SSH_WAIT` seconds, default 300), `up` keeps the record and recovery bundle, exits `UBI_CREATE_UNKNOWN`, and leaves the VM to its TTL. `run` reports `left=unknown` for it instead of `left=0`. (GST-1463)
