- **An owner's ruling now closes a finding on a round-2 full pass and on a Step 11 voice, as the review policy says, not only on a granted fix-check.** docs/review-policy.md promised that a loop at its round cap finishes with "an owner-ruled note … closed and listed", but only finish's fix-check chain read rulings, so a full pass blocked on one ruled finding stayed `UNVERIFIED` and a Step 11 finding no row recorded stayed `unrecorded` (garrytan/gstack#4248, #4266, #4257; GST-2273). Now `gstack-review-read` applies each approved ruling whose head lies inside the row's reviewed range, and `gstack-review-scope summary` counts a ruled blocker as noted. A ruling can name a finding by fingerprint or ledger id. The binding rules stay: a ruling outside the range closes nothing, the lane's own row fields rule nothing, gate findings are never ruled, and an edit after the bound head still needs review. A stopped read binds only if its commit didn't move while it ran, so an A→B→A read no longer binds as one tree. On a fix-check, a finding it read as fixed no longer holds it open, and `FIX_CHECK_OPEN` also names findings no ruling can close. Approval reads now include the mounted Capy Drive mirror without `GSTACK_CAPY=1`, so rulings look the same in every shell.
