**`/eval-plan` lets a judge that grades code be checked against a third model's labels instead of people's (R126).** A plan's `## Scoring` can now say `labels-by: google/gemini-4-neon` (another third family, such as `x-ai/grok-4.7`, when Gemini is the judge or an arm) with `label-kind: code`, `blind: yes`, and frozen known-answer anchors (`anchors:`, `anchor-pass:` and the `anchors-digest:` digest that `gstack-eval-plan anchors --hash` prints). `judge --check --anchors` counts the labeller's labels only after it meets its anchor pass line, and `results --check` wants the results to say "judge checked against <model>, a third model, not against people". Judges of anything other than code, quality and taste included, still need people's or construction labels, and the label count and kappa bar are unchanged.
