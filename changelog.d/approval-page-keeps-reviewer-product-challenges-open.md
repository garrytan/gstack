- **/autoplan's approval page no longer hides a reviewer's product objection behind "keep your direction".** A plan can now quote its owner under `### Owner's words`, and only those quotes count as the plan's direction. When a reviewer recommends changing anything else the plan says (its scope, build order, direction, cost or packaging), at any severity and even from one reviewer, the approval page shows it as a lettered choice that recommends the reviewer's change, and the cost line says what taking it actually does. A plan with no Owner's words section keeps today's default and gets one question asking the owner to confirm the direction is theirs. A challenge raised in an earlier review round stays on the page until the owner answers it or a later round marks it fixed. App plans with a `### Day one` table (the owner's first actions, how often, the feature, the way in from the first screen, the first build that has it, and how it's checked) show it first on the approval page, turn each guessed row into a question for the owner, and letter every cut that drops a day-one row or a quoted owner line; an app plan without the table gets one item asking for it. Declining a reviewer's change on a High or Critical finding keeps the plan as written and carries the finding into the build as a blocking task. Quoted owner words, including ones a later revision removed, are redacted from public exports.
