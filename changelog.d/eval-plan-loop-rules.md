**`/eval-plan` now writes eval plans that waste less money.** `gstack-eval-plan prereg --check` refuses a plan frozen on or after 2026-11-21 unless it pilots every arm on at most a tenth of its budget (drawing every unit type, in a seeded order), checks its judge on each arm's outputs at a stated reasoning effort, carries a cost line, scores any "smaller change" arm with a regression-test bar, says how task machines were checked for readable answers, and, above $500 paid, preregisters one look at half the units with O'Brien-Fleming and futility bounds. `judge --check` reads agreement per arm. A small question gets a quick look (`prereg --init --quick`): at most $100, three lines, and no published number, which `results --check` enforces. New plans name GPT-6.2 Sol as the frontier Sol, and GPT-6.2 Sol and Astra are priced. Older frozen plans keep their rules and models.
