### Fixed
- **`/eval-plan` pilots with a subscription arm can now be approved, and the cost projection is per arm (#3653).** `gstack-eval-plan pilot start --seat-metered <arm>` declares an arm that runs on a subscription with no extra charge (for example `claude -p` on a seat). Its attempts hold none of the spend cap, settle as `unknown` (a real charge still counts as spent), and get their own `PILOT_SEAT_METERED:` line, so the verdict covers the arms that cost money. `pilot report` now projects each arm from its own attempts (one `PILOT_ARM:` line each) and adds them up. Before, it averaged all arms together: a priced arm's cost was multiplied across arms with no price, and two arms at different prices got a band so wide that a pilot that fit the cap was told it exceeded it. The skill also says to stop a seat-metered run at its first quota or rate-limit error, with no retry (idea from #3273 by @merit-blake).
