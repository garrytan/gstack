### Fixed

- **Lead judgment can lower a native adversarial finding to the Severity its reader stated beside `Blocks: no` (GST-1406).** When the native reader wrote its `Severity` and `Blocks: no` after prose (`… so the docs are wrong. Severity: Medium. Blocks: no.`), collect kept the heading's FIXABLE as CRITICAL. Judge refused any lowering, so the native record never resolved and /ship's Step 11.5 couldn't bind. Collect still can't tell those fields from another subject's written under the same heading, so it keeps the label. Now `gstack-review-dispatch judge` lets lead judgment lower such a finding to its stated Severity, never below, with a reason. It's recorded as `dismissed`, and the ledger's blocker count follows it. When the native reader writes those fields after prose in the heading's own paragraph, collect now keeps them on the heading's record (at its label) instead of splitting them into a second record. Other readers, a finding that states `Blocks: yes`, no `Blocks` or a Severity that isn't a severity (such as `Severity: FIXABLE`), and a lowering below the stated Severity or with no reason are still refused as downgrades. See docs/review-policy.md.
