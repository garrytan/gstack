- **The `browse handoff` window no longer opens with a stray blank tab.** Every handoff window showed the page to sign in to plus an empty `about:blank` tab next to it, which `browse tabs` didn't list and no browse command could close (#4323). The new persistent context opens a first tab of its own, and `restoreState()` opened the saved tabs without ever closing it. Now, once the saved tabs are restored, any context page that browse doesn't track and that is still on `about:blank` is closed. Tracked tabs, and untracked pages that have navigated somewhere, are left alone.
