**/ship's scoped second pass runs again, and its readers review the fix range instead of the whole branch.** After a blocking fix on a small range, `gstack-review-plan` plans a scoped pass: one fix-check, the Red Team and the native adversarial pass on the range. `gstack-review-dispatch pass` refused that plan with `BRIEF_RENDER_FAILED: a pass dispatches no fix-check reader`, because a fix-check is not a pass reader. Every scoped pass failed this way, and GEVA-82 rendered its briefs by hand to get through (GST-619). Even past that refusal, the pass would have staged its readers on the merge base, because the Capy pass program never passes `--diff-base`.

### Fixed
- **`gstack-review-dispatch pass` keeps the fix-check out of the pass manifest and prints it as `FIX_CHECK: planned effort=full (Step 3.5 runs it on its own; not in this manifest)`.** A resumed pass prints the line again. Step 9's decision text in /ship now says to run that fix-check as Step 3.5 beside the scoped pass. No reader is added or removed: the pass runs the readers the plan already lists.
- **A scoped pass's diff base is the plan's fix range base.** `pass` resolves it from the plan's `fix_range`, so the Red Team, the voices and audit readers review `RANGE_BASE..HEAD`, as docs/review-policy.md describes. An explicit `--diff-base` still wins, and a scoped plan without a readable range base is refused.

### Tests
- `test/review-pass.test.ts`: GEVA-2's invocation (`--extras`, `--pass1-depth standard --fixed-blocking --range-base <short sha>`) plans `NEXT: scoped`. `pass` exits 0, stages the Red Team, the adversarial pass, the outside voice and the audit reader on the full range-base sha, prints the `FIX_CHECK:` line, and prints it again on resume. It fails on main with `BRIEF_RENDER_FAILED`, and on the fix-check change alone because the manifest's diff base is the merge base.
