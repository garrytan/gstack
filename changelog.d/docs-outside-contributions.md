- **CONTRIBUTING.md now says plainly how outside reports and PRs land.** It explains that AI agents working for the maintainer triage, rebuild and land outside patches with the author credited, which is why commits read as the maintainer's. It lists the three paths a PR can take (a docs-only PR for people can merge as it is after its links, commands and install steps are checked; a plainly correct fix of about 40 lines is taken even when small; everything else is rebuilt on main when it has proof, a real consequence and matters to most users), the 48-hour first-reply goal, and the narrowed open-PR limit, which never applies to anyone with a PR taken in the last 90 days. The stale wave process, which merged outside PRs on a collector branch, is gone. `crew/docs/captain.md`, the Captain's CREW template and `/captain` match.
