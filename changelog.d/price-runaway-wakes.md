- **`gstack-runaway` now says what a thread's cold and empty wakes cost, not just how many there were (GST-2770).** Each wake it reads is marked cold when it started more than 5 minutes after the thread's last activity, so the prompt cache had lapsed, and is priced in warm model calls, with a cold call at 19.4 (measured on Opus 5.5: $2.42 against $0.125 per million counted tokens; a call after a tool that ran past 5 minutes is cold too). The RUNAWAY line adds `wakes`, `cold_wakes`, `empty_wakes`, `cold_extra_spend` and `empty_spend`, the empty-wake warning gives the empty wakes' share of spend, and `scan --wakes` prints one WAKE line per wake. A message sent to another thread now counts as action even when its state is "working", and more than 5 minutes idle inside a turn (not counting a tool's own run time) starts a new wake. On four coordinating threads over 4.7 days the meter's cold and empty shares came within 1.3 points of a hand count; before this, sends were counted as empty and the empty share read 4 to 12 points high. /runaway-stopper's fetch program now keeps each message's target and each call's run time.
