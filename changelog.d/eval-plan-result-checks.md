- `/eval-plan` now runs four result checks instead of leaving them to judgment. `prereg --check` requires a `## Decisions` section (what each possible result changes, at least two different actions) and a `## Scoring` section (a judge from another model family than every arm, checked against at least 40 hand labels at kappa 0.6 or better, and paired results with a 95% interval and the smallest detectable difference stated before the run). The new `judge --check --labels` scores the judge against hand labels, and `results --check` holds back a judged result without passing labels, a zero from a counter never shown to find a planted case, and a failures column with no answer-only column beside it. Preregistrations frozen before 2026-11-15 keep their rules.
