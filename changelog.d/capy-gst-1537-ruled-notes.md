- **A finding the owner rules a disclosed note no longer keeps a granted fix-check from counting, and every ruling is listed.** On qm#3122 at 532cc5c the owner ruled two CRITICALs notes, not fixes. The runner logged them as `skipped`, `finish` counted a skipped CRITICAL as open, so the granted fix-check row (its own findings all below the floor) never read CURRENT and `finish` stopped with "behavior changed since review". The owner now records each ruling with `gstack-approvals rule --finding <fingerprint> --head <stop head> --reason <why> --words <exact words> --by <who ruled>`: an approved approval row with the ruler, the head, the reason and the time. `rule` refuses a head that is not a commit, a multi-line reason, a `--by` naming gstack and any ruling inside the checkout's open /ship run, and `add` refuses the ruling scope. `finish` reads rulings from the approval record only (a `ruled` field or `action: "ruled"` on the lane's own review-log row is ignored), treats a ruled finding as closed when the ruling's head lies inside the fix-check's range, whichever came first, and prints each as `RULED_NOTE:`; a ruling at an earlier head is listed as not applied. A skipped CRITICAL with no ruling stays open. `gstack-approvals rulings` prints the `Ruled note: …` lines for the Pre-Landing Review and the PR body; the receipt records them as `ruled_notes`, `gstack-ship-receipt write --body` refuses a body missing one, and the READY block prints each. See docs/ship-policy.md#ruled-notes. (GST-1537)
