**`/cso` now reports its full list of plausible security findings instead of stopping at one or two.** Every plausible defect goes on the list, with no count cap: findings the code fully traces are reported at medium or high confidence, and everything else, however many questions stay open, is listed as a low-confidence hypothesis that says what would confirm it. A challenge changes a finding's confidence but no longer decides whether it is reported, the final table lists every submitted finding with the likeliest first, and a short list is no longer a reason to end the audit early. On 52 real security advisories, `/cso` reported 1.7 findings per audit against 14.8 for a plain audit request, with about the same share of real hits (gstack-evals#447).
