**`/cso` now reports its full list of plausible security findings instead of stopping at one or two.** Every plausible defect goes on the list, with no count cap: findings the code fully traces are reported at medium or high confidence, and everything else, however many questions stay open, is listed as a low-confidence hypothesis that says what would confirm it. A challenge changes a finding's confidence but no longer decides whether it is reported, candidates left unchallenged are submitted as hypotheses before the audit finishes, the final tables list every supported finding, likeliest first, and every hypothesis, and the audit keeps going while its helper reports more than two minutes left, through every selected phase, judging time only from the helper's clock, so a short list no longer ends it early. On 52 real security advisories, `/cso` reported 1.7 findings per audit against 14.8 for a plain audit request, with about the same share of real hits (gstack-evals#447).
To make room within the skill's size budget, `/cso`'s instructions no longer repeat two storage details its helper enforces on its own: public archive caches stay hash-verified and capped at 10 GiB, and repair candidates and bundles still keep the redacted request, patch, observations, provenance, input hashes and assurance labels that replay needs, and the watchdog still cleans up run-owned resources after a cancellation, deadline or lost agent.
