### Fixed

- **Code a reviewer quotes in a fence no longer turns into findings (GST-1409).** When a reader quoted a probe input or a checklist inside a ``` or ~~~ fence, `gstack-review-dispatch collect` parsed each quoted line. A quoted `### 1. FIXABLE: …` heading or a quoted `Blocks: yes` became a phantom CRITICAL that lead judgment couldn't lower, and the real finding lost its own Severity and Blocks fields. Now a fence that opens inside a finding is that finding's body: its lines collect nothing, and the record notes `(code block: <n> lines)`. A JSON finding line inside a fence still collects, and a fence with no finding open parses as before. Checked against 334 stored reader outputs (668 comparisons): 664 collect the same, and the 4 that change each drop only fenced quotes.
