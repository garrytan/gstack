**gstack now writes up a failed or shelved attempt before closing it.** Any experiment, eval arm, prototype or PR that closes as failed, shelved, cut, null or not now first gets a short write-up (takeaways, what worked, what didn't with its numbers, why it stopped, what would reopen it, where the data is, cost), and the close links it. The template is docs/shelved-attempt.md; the factory page, ETHOS.md's "Why null?" verdict, /eval-plan's results step, /captain's report and close rules, and the lane report template all point at it, and `gstack-archive-check thread` prints a `NOTE:` for each PR that closed without merging so the archive step checks the link.
