`gstack-design-lint` now names a path it cannot read relative to the folder you ran it from, even when you reach that path through a symlink. Before, a missing file, a dangling link or a link that points at itself under a symlinked folder (on macOS, anything under `/tmp` or `/var`) printed its full absolute path. The design-lint tests also pass on a Mac again, where four of them compared paths against the unresolved temp folder. Thanks to Akkyman2 for the report and first fix (#4856).
